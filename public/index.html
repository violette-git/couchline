FAQ

Good questions.

Does it work with Netflix and Hulu?

Yes. On a computer with the Couchline extension, play, pause, and seek sync on their own, and the room rolls on with the next episode. On phones, each of you plays on your own account and a shared countdown starts you together. The Shows tab remembers where you are in a series.

Do we need accounts?

No. Start a room, send the link, and each person picks a name. Your queue, chat, and shows are saved in your browser.

How many people can watch?

Rooms have no size limit. The video call shows up to five cameras, and everyone past that joins with audio.

Who can join my room?

Private rooms (the default) are only for people with the code or link, and never show up anywhere. Public rooms are listed in Public rooms while someone is in them, and anyone can join.

What happens with YouTube ads?

Ads play separately for each person. Couchline catches whoever watched an ad back up afterward. YouTube Premium skips them entirely.

Will the call work on cell data?

Usually. Some cell and work networks block direct calls; adding a relay server on the host fixes that. Watching works either way.
