FAQ

Good questions.

Can Couchline control Netflix or Hulu?

No website can, so you each play on your own account and screen. Couchline runs a shared countdown so you press play at the same moment, and the Shows tab remembers which episode you’re on.

Do we need accounts?

No. Start a room, send the link, and each of you picks a name. Your queue and shows are saved in your browser.

Who can join my room?

Anyone with the link. Room codes are random and hard to guess, but there’s no password yet, so share it only with the people you’re watching with.

What happens with YouTube ads?

Ads play separately for each of you. Couchline catches whoever watched an ad back up afterward. YouTube Premium skips them entirely.

Will the call work on cell data?

Usually. Some cell and work networks block direct calls; adding a relay server on the host fixes that. Watching still works either way.
