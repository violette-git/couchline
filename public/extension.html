The Couchline extension

Watch Netflix and Hulu in sync, keep the call and chat right beside the show, and add videos from anywhere with one click.

Extensions only work on a computer. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on your laptop or desktop to install it.

On your phone you can still add things without copying links: open a room’s menu and see “Add things from your phone’s apps”.

This works in Chrome and Edge. Open this page in one of those to install it. Everything else in Couchline works in any browser.

Download the extension

A small .zip file. Free.

Netflix and Hulu in sync

Press play, pause, or skip ahead on Netflix or Hulu and everyone follows. When the next episode starts, the room moves along with you.

The room beside the show

A sidebar on the page with your video call, chat, and reactions, so you never leave the show.

Add from anywhere

A Couchline button on YouTube, Vimeo, Twitch, and Instagram, and “Add to Couchline” when you right-click any link.

Share your scrolling

On Instagram, turn on Share my scrolling and everyone sees the reel you land on, on any device.

Install it in about a minute

  1. Download and unzip

    Download the file above, then unzip it (on Windows, right-click it and choose Extract All). Keep the couchline-extension folder somewhere it can stay, like Documents. The browser loads the extension from that folder.

  2. Open your extensions page

    Type this into the address bar and press Enter. Browsers don’t let websites open this page for you.

    chrome://extensions

  3. Turn on Developer mode

    It’s a switch in the top right corner of that page.

  4. Load unpacked

    Click Load unpacked and choose the couchline-extension folder (the folder itself, not a file inside it).

  5. Pin it and pick your room

    Click the puzzle piece in the toolbar and pin Couchline. Click it, add your room code and your name, and press Save. Your Couchline address is already filled in.

Good to know

  • Everyone who wants Netflix and Hulu sync needs it. Sync turns on when everyone watching has the extension; otherwise Couchline uses a shared countdown.
  • Updating: when there’s a new version, download it again, unzip it over the old folder, and click the reload arrow on Couchline in your extensions page.
  • Why Developer mode? Couchline isn’t in the Chrome Web Store yet, so the browser asks you to switch this on to install it yourself. It only affects extensions you load this way.
  • Privacy: the extension only talks to this Couchline site. It runs on Netflix, Hulu, YouTube, Vimeo, Twitch, and Instagram pages, and only shares what you choose to share with your room.
